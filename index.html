📲 How to transfer routes to Garmin Venu 2 Plus?

  1. Click "Copy Route String" above to copy the compressed Polyline.
  2. Open the Garmin Connect or Connect IQ Store app on your smartphone.
  3. Navigate to My Devices ➔ Tap Venu 2 Plus ➔ Activities, Apps & More ➔ Data Fields.
  4. Locate He-Kim-Koo (Route Field) ➔ Open Settings.
  5. Paste the copied string into Route String (Polyline), choose Route Direction (Forward or Reverse), optionally customize Map View Range (Meters), and tap Save.
  6. Start a "Run" activity on the watch; your GPS art will instantly render with real-time navigation!
